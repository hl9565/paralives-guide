Is Paralives worth it?

The short version: Paralives is easiest to recommend to players who specifically want a fresh life sim and are comfortable with Early Access rough edges. If you need a finished game, broad content depth, or years of expansion packs, waiting is the calmer choice.

Is Paralives released?

Yes. The official Paralives FAQ says Paralives released in Early Access on May 25, 2026.

Where can I buy Paralives?

The official FAQ says Paralives is available on Steam for PC and Mac.

Is Paralives multiplayer?

No. The official FAQ presents Paralives as a single-player game.

Does Paralives have mods?

Yes. Paralives has a Steam Workshop hub, and the site's mods guide explains the current Workshop-based setup.
