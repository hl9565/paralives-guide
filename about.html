About

Updated: 2026-05-27 Game Version: EA 0.1 About

This site is a small, independent fan resource for people who want Paralives coverage with structure, context, and fewer recycled rumors. The aim is simple: make it easier to find what is confirmed, what is still changing, and where the gaps still are.

Who runs this site

Paralives Guide is maintained as a focused editorial project rather than a community wiki. That means the voice can stay consistent, the structure can stay tight, and every new page has to earn its place instead of appearing because a placeholder was easy to publish.

How we verify facts

The source ladder is public on purpose: official Paralives Studio posts first, then the Steam Early Access page, then official Discord patch-note posts, then established games press when it adds useful context. If a gameplay detail cannot be confirmed from that chain or from direct hands-on checking, the article leaves a [FILL IN] marker or a visible Status: note instead of smoothing over the gap.

For a short routing layer built on that same rule, start with the site's Paralives FAQ before jumping into a deeper guide.

What we don't do

No scraped wiki dumps. No roadmap features written as if they already shipped. No paywalled basics, and no comment-section spectacle standing in for reporting. If a topic would be more noise than help, it waits.

How this site is funded

The plan is to fund hosting and maintenance through Google AdSense once the site has enough real pages to justify it. This project is not operated by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Paralives Studio; the footer disclaimer is not legal filler, it is the plain truth.
