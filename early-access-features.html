Paralives Early Access Features: Day-One Systems and Planned Updates

Updated: 2026-05-27 Game Version: EA 0.1 Feature overview

If you want the shortest version, Paralives officially separates what is playable on day one of Early Access from what is planned to arrive later as free updates. That distinction matters because a planned feature is not the same thing as a launch feature.

This guide uses the official development page and FAQ only, so it stays close to the studio's own wording instead of mixing confirmed systems with wishlist assumptions.

Status: Official source guide based on the current Paralives development page and FAQ. Early Access begins on May 25, 2026.

Quick answer

On the official development page, Paralives uses two clear buckets: Features available on day one of Early Access and Planned to come out during Early Access. If you are checking whether a system is there at launch, that heading is the safest filter to use.

That means systems like personality traits, emotions, flexible walls, and multiple outfits are presented as day-one features, while seasons, pets, pools, town editing, and more advanced tools are listed for later during Early Access.

What the official pages confirm

Release timingEarly Access starts on May 25, 2026.
Where to playSteam for PC and Mac, according to the official FAQ.
Main distinctionDay-one features are separate from features planned to come later during Early Access.
Useful cautionDo not treat planned features as already playable just because they are on the roadmap.

Paramaker

Paramaker is also in the day-one bucket, with core appearance and outfit tools available from the start. The later bucket covers pet creation, deeper genetics editing, and broader content expansion rather than the basic creator itself.

Day one of Early Access

Paramaker systems at launch

  • Color wheel for everything
  • Height slider
  • Body and face sliders
  • Tattoo placement tool
  • Layering and mixing of tops, such as an undershirt with a jacket
  • Asymmetry, including eye color, socks, and more
  • Genetics system
  • Creating and managing multiple outfits
Planned during Early Access

Paramaker systems listed for later

  • Creating pets in Paramaker
  • Editing the genetics of a Para with advanced tools
  • More clothing items, hairstyles, and accessories

What not to assume yet

The safest reading is simple: if a feature appears under the official "planned during Early Access" heading, do not talk about it like it is already live on May 25, 2026. That applies to pets, seasons, family trees, cars and bikes, gardening and fishing, pools, basements, town editing, and the more advanced creation tools.

The FAQ also helps keep scope grounded. Paralives is listed as a single-player game that does not require an internet connection, there is no open beta planned, and the Early Access release is on Steam for PC and Mac. Those details are useful context, but they do not change the central rule: roadmap items are not the same as launch features.

FAQ

What features are available on day one of Paralives Early Access?

According to Paralives' official development page, day-one Early Access includes core live mode systems, build mode tools, and Paramaker features such as traits, emotions, needs, flexible walls, stairs, fences, body sliders, and multiple outfits.

Are pets, seasons, cars, and family trees available on day one?

No. The official development page lists weather and seasons, family tree, dogs, cats and horses, and cars and bikes as planned to come out during Early Access rather than available on day one.

Is build mode available at the start of Early Access?

Yes. Paralives lists build mode features such as the color wheel, flexible wall placement, split-level platforms, multiple floors, object resizing, stairs, roof placement, and fences as available on day one of Early Access.

Is Paramaker available at the start of Early Access?

Yes. The official development page lists Paramaker features such as height sliders, body and face sliders, tattoo placement, layered tops, asymmetry, genetics, and multiple outfits as day-one Early Access features.

Where is Paralives available in Early Access?

The official FAQ says Paralives releases in Early Access on May 25, 2026 and is available on Steam for PC and Mac.
