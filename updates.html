What counts as verified here

Official Paralives pages count as the highest-confidence source layer for this page. Reviewed site changes also count, because they are visible in the public pages you can browse here. What does not count as official update material is just as important: unverified community tips, fan rumor chains, and screenshot fragments without an official source do not become patch notes on this page.

Later, this route can grow into a fuller update log that references reviewed source-monitor digests and cleaner patch-history summaries. For now, the priority is being stable and honest, not pretending the automation layer is already finished.
