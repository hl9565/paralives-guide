Paralives FAQ: Early Access Questions Answered

Updated: 2026-05-29 Game Version: EA 0.1 FAQ

This Paralives FAQ collects the questions new readers ask first, then routes each one to the cleanest source or the most relevant guide on this site. The aim is not to pad a giant checklist, but to give short answers that stay honest about what is confirmed, what is still evolving in Early Access, and what the official pages have not pinned down.

Buying & platform

Storefronts, devices, and platform questions

Is Paralives released?

Check the official Paralives FAQ or the Steam store page for the current price, because store pricing can change.

Where can I buy Paralives?

Early Access

What Early Access changes about the answers

Is Paralives in Early Access?

Yes. Paralives is in Early Access, not full release, and the official FAQ lists May 25, 2026 as the Early Access launch date.

Will the price increase after Early Access?

Check the official Paralives FAQ for the current pricing policy, because that is the safest place to confirm whether the studio still plans any price change after Early Access.

Will my save work after the full release?

There is no official promise on final save compatibility on the cited pages here, so treat future save-transfer details as not yet confirmed.

Gameplay

What is in the game now, and what is planned later

Does Paralives have an open world town?

Yes. The site's Early Access features guide points to the official development page, which lists an open world town as a day-one live mode feature.

Does Paralives age characters?

Yes. The official development page, as summarized in the site's Early Access features guide, lists aging and death as day-one live mode systems.

Does Paralives have toddlers / babies?

The official development page says having children is part of day-one live mode, but this FAQ does not add extra stage-by-stage detail beyond what the official pages confirm.

Does Paralives have careers?

Yes. The official development page lists work as rabbit holes and career progression as day-one live mode features.

Does Paralives have pets?

Not on day one of Early Access. The official development page lists dogs, cats and horses as planned to come later during Early Access.

Does Paralives have weather?

Not on day one of Early Access. The official development page lists weather and seasons as planned to come later during Early Access.

Is Paralives better than The Sims?

That depends on what you want from a life sim. This site does not name a winner here, because Paralives is still in Early Access and feature priorities differ from player to player. The dedicated Paralives vs The Sims 4 comparison breaks down the tradeoffs in more detail.

Does Paralives need an internet connection?

No. The official FAQ says an internet connection is not required to play.
