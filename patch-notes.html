Updated: 2026-05-29 Game Version: EA 0.1 Page type: Patch archive

All Paralives Early Access patches, grouped by version, with official sources.

This page is the version-by-version companion to updates.html. The goal is simple: point to the original official announcement, keep the summary neutral, and avoid reposting the full patch notes body when the source already exists.

Methodology: Every entry on this page comes from official Steam announcements and official paralives.com pages only. If an announcement does not expose an exact version number, this archive keeps the slot broad and marks the missing label instead of inventing one.

How this archive is sourced

The source chain is intentionally narrow: official Steam Community Announcements, plus official pages on paralives.com when they carry the same release information. Community summaries, news roundups, and player-made change logs do not get promoted into archive entries here.

Archive rules

Allowed sourcesOfficial Steam announcements and official paralives.com pages only.
Entry formatVersion label, release date, neutral summary, and an official source link.
Missing version labelsIf the source does not show one clearly, the archive keeps a broader version bucket and says so.
Editorial stanceOrganization and summary only, not a repost of the complete patch notes text.
