Houses

This hub is reserved for community house plans once the submission flow is stable enough to protect both creators and readers. The point is not volume; it is a gallery you can trust to credit work properly and avoid reposting someone else's build without consent.

Status: PLANNED · The house gallery opens after the submission flow is designed in Phase 4.

Trust rules are visible from day one: every entry gets manual review, creator credit, and a reverse-image-search pass before publish. That slows the gallery down, but it also keeps the eventual archive worth browsing.
