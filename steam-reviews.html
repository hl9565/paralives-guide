What this means

Steam's Very Positive label signals that the large majority of player reviews are positive. It is a strong reception tier for a paid game, while still leaving room below Steam's highest praise tier.

For a buyer, that usually means the audience response is more encouraging than mixed or uncertain, but it still should be read alongside the game's current Early Access state, your tolerance for unfinished systems, and the kind of life-sim experience you want today.

Context for life-sim genre

Within a life-sim context, 89% Very Positive is a healthy signal. It suggests that many players already feel good enough about the current build to leave a favorable review, even with the extra scrutiny that follows an Early Access launch.

That does not automatically settle the buying question, though. A potential buyer still needs to weigh current reception against price, roadmap timing, and whether the unfinished parts of the game line up with what they personally care about most.

Methodology and freshness

This page is a single locked snapshot from 2026-05-29. Steam review counts and percentages can move every day, so use the live Paralives Steam store page if you want the current real-time number before buying.
