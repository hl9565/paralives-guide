Paramaker guide

Updated: 2026-05-27 Game Version: EA 0.1 Reference guide

Paramaker is the part of Paralives where a new Para starts to feel like a person instead of a blank preset. This guide is here to orient you without pretending every old preview clip equals a shipped feature.

What Paramaker is for

At the simplest level, Paramaker is the game's character creator. It is where you shape appearance, identity, and the broad frame of who a Para is before everyday life takes over in live mode.

That sounds ordinary on paper, but it matters because character creators in life sims tend to become shorthand for the whole game's tone. If the creator feels flexible and readable, the rest of the simulation usually feels easier to trust.

How the creator is described publicly

Official studio material presents Paramaker as a flexible creator built around expressive customization rather than a tiny set of locked presets. That high-level promise is solid enough to write down. The fragile part is any exact list of sliders, panels, or sub-menus unless it has been confirmed in the public build.

For that reason, this guide stays broad on purpose. It aims to help you understand the creator's shape, not to fake certainty about every click path.

Where traits and personality fit

Character creation in a life sim is not just a face-maker; it is also the point where future social chemistry starts to take shape. Traits matter here because they eventually affect how a Para behaves, who they clash with, and how a household feels once you leave the creator.

The site will break that out properly once the first verified trait rows exist. Until then, the clean next stop is the planned Database hub, which is reserved for a traits-first rollout rather than a made-up full list. A calm way to approach a first Para

Start with the broad silhouette of the person you want to play, then layer details in a second pass. In practice that usually means landing the overall look first, then circling back to name, traits, and any contextual details that only make sense once the character already feels grounded.

This is also where life-sim muscle memory can help or mislead. If you come from The Sims, some instincts will transfer, but it is worth leaving room for Paralives to structure personality a little differently once the build settles.

What still needs direct build checks

Any exact option counts, hidden compatibility rules, or claims about how trait combinations behave in live play should be treated as open until tested. That is not a weakness in the guide; it is the point of the guide. Clear uncertainty is better than confident drift.
